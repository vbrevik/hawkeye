# Kazhymukan Munaitpasov Stadium (Astana)

_Sports venue in Kazakhstan_

Kajymukan Munaitpasov Stadium is a multi-purpose stadium in Astana, Kazakhstan. It is named after the Kazakh wrestler Kazhymukan Munaitpasov. He was the first Kazakh who became a world champion. The stadium is currently used mostly for association football matches and is the home stadium of FC Zhenis. The stadium holds 12,350 people.
