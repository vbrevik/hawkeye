# Keny (futsal player)

_Equatoguinean futsal player_

Kennedy Ofong Ubenga, known as Keny, is an Equatoguinean futsal player who plays for Manzanares as a winger and the Equatorial Guinea national futsal team. He also holds Spanish citizenship.
