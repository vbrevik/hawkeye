# Hassi Bel Guebour

_Village in Illizi Province, Algeria_

Hassi Bel Guebour is a village in the commune of Bordj Omar Driss, in In Amenas District, Illizi Province, Algeria. It is located at the intersection of the N3 national highway and the local road to Bordj Omar Driss.
