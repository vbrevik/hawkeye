# Xiaomi 13T Pro

_2023 flagship smartphone by Xiaomi running HyperOS_

Xiaomi 13T Pro is a premium Android smartphone developed by Xiaomi and part of the Xiaomi T Series. It was officially announced and released on September 26, 2023 , and serves as the successor to the Xiaomi 12T Pro. The 13T Pro introduces several flagship-level upgrades including a high-refresh AMOLED display, Leica-tuned triple camera setup, and ultra-fast 120W charging.
