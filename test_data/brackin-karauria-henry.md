# Brackin Karauria-Henry

_New Zealand rugby player_

Brackin Karauria-Henry is a New Zealand rugby footballer who has played rugby sevens for Australia. As of 2018 he plays for Japanese Top League club NTT Com Shining Arcs. He previously played Super Rugby for the Brumbies and NSW Waratahs. His usual position is centre. He competed for Japan 2020 Summer Olympics.
