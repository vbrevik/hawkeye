# Expelled from Paradise

_2014 Japanese animated science fiction film_

Expelled from Paradise  is a 2014 Japanese anime science fiction film. The film is directed by Seiji Mizushima, with a screenplay written by Gen Urobuchi. The film was produced by Toei Animation and animated by Graphinica, and was distributed by T-Joy.
