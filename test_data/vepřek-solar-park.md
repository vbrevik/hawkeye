# Vepřek Solar Park

_Photovoltaic power plant in the Czech Republic_

The Vepřek Solar Park is a photovoltaic power plant in the Czech Republic. It is situated near Vepřek village, part of Nová Ves municipality, approx 30 km north from Prague.
