# Lyon Villeurbanne XIII

_French semi-professional rugby league club_

Lyon Villeurbanne XIII are a semi-professional rugby league club from the town of Lyon, Rhône-Alpes in France. Formed in 1934 they are one of the oldest clubs in France. During the 1950s they won both the league and cup. Their home stadium is the Stade Georges Lyvet. They withdrew from the Elite 2 Championship, the second tier of French rugby league, before the start of the 2021–2022 season.
