# Delmonico steak

_Preparation of beef popularized in New York City_

Delmonico steak is a name used for several cuts of beef, usually ribeye, cut thickly. It was popularized by Delmonico's restaurant in New York City from the mid-19th century. Currently the steak of that name sold by Delmonico's is a thick-cut ribeye.
