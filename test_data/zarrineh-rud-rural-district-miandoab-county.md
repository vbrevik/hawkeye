# Zarrineh Rud Rural District (Miandoab County)

_Rural district in West Azerbaijan province, Iran_

Zarrineh Rud Rural District is in Baktash District of Miandoab County, West Azerbaijan province, Iran. Its capital is the village of Satelmish-e Mohammadlu. The previous capital of the rural district was the village of Baktash, now a city.
