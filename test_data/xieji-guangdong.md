# Xieji, Guangdong

_Town in Guangdong, People's Republic of China_

Xieji is a town under the administration of Gaozhou City in southwestern Guangdong province, China, located about 14 kilometres (8.7 mi) east of downtown Gaozhou. As of 2011, it has one residential community (居委会) and 19 villages under its administration.
